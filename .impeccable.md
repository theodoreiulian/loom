## Design Context

### Users
Primary audience: visual creators exploring AI image and video workflows, from first ideas to connected creative experiments. Speak directly to their curiosity with approachable language, tangible examples and an easy first step.

Secondary audience: professional designers and creative studios exploring concepts, visual directions and product campaigns. Show practical creative applications without making the page feel technical or enterprise-focused.

### Brand Personality
Imaginative, tactile, open. Write in plain, conversational language. Describe real creative tasks and how the editor works; avoid forced punchlines, grand promises and repeated slogans.

### Aesthetic Direction
The creator requested liquid glass, reactive interactions and unique design elements. Use a bright pearlescent studio: warm ivory, translucent lavender glass, charcoal type, expressive italic serif accents and flowing node connections.

### Design Principles
- Show the workflow as a creative material: ideas become connected nodes.
- Lead with creative exploration; use campaign and concept examples to also speak to designers and studios.
- Keep the real editor one click away from every main call to action.
- Use glass to reveal depth and connections, while keeping text readable.
- Make interaction purposeful, keyboard accessible and respectful of reduced motion.
- Explain provider keys, generation costs and browser storage plainly.

- Keep the visual design expressive and the copy grounded. Prefer specific examples and useful instructions over metaphors about sparks, magic or possibility.
